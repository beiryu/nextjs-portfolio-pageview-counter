## 📚 **Technology Stack**

Typescript, React, TailwindCSS, Vite, Shadcn/UI, Vercel, PWA

## 🗃️ Features

- No registration required
- Mark completed days
- Track your progress
- Maintain a journal for each habit
- Achievements to motivate and celebrate your progress
