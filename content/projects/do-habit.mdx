## Technology Stack

TypeScript, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, PWA, Vercel

## Overview

Own Habit is a habit tracker built around a single deliberate constraint: **no registration, no account, no backend.**

Habit trackers have the worst funnel in consumer software. Someone decides to change something about their life, opens an app, and is immediately asked to create an account — and a meaningful share of them never come back. Removing that step entirely means the product is usable within a second of the page loading, and the person is marking their first day before they would otherwise have finished typing an email address.

The trade is real and accepted: no sync across devices, no cloud backup. In exchange, the product has no signup friction, no privacy question to answer, no data to breach, and no server cost.

## Features

- **No registration** — open it and start
- **Daily completion marking** with a clear, fast interaction, since this is the action repeated every single day and everything else is secondary
- **Streak and progress tracking**, visualised so the pattern is visible at a glance
- **Per-habit journalling** — a note attached to each habit, because *why* a streak broke is more useful than the fact that it did
- **Achievements** to mark meaningful milestones
- **Installable** as a progressive web app, so it sits on the home screen and launches like a native app
- **Fully offline** — the app works with no connection at all, which is exactly when someone is most likely to check off a morning habit

## Engineering

- **Local persistence** as the single source of truth, with the schema versioned so a future update cannot corrupt existing data
- **Service worker** caching the application shell for instant, offline-capable launches
- **shadcn/ui on Radix primitives**, so the interactive controls are keyboard and screen-reader accessible without reimplementation
- **Vite build** producing a small enough bundle that first load is effectively instant
- **No analytics, no tracking, no third-party requests** — a claim the network tab can verify

A small product with an opinion. The engineering interest is in how much can be removed — accounts, servers, sync, tracking — while the product still does its job completely.
